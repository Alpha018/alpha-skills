Multi-tenant SaaS backend, around 40 tenant organizations total (bounded, known list, added rarely by an internal admin process). Every request depends on TenantDataSourceService (request-scoped), and most of the rest of the module graph transitively depends on it too. Latency has been climbing under load, and profiling shows most of the time going into re-constructing this request-scoped graph on every single request even though the same tenant hits the API repeatedly.
